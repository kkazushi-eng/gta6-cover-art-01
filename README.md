Vice Panels turns your photos and a caption into a GTA-style multi-panel poster: pick a layout, a sunset or neon colour grade, crop and place each panel, then export a 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080 PNG — all offline in your browser, with your photos never uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
